Our Services
On boarding &amp; Compliance: iFinserve streamlines your entry into the Indian financial ecosystem
with expert guidance on Demat account openings, PAN acquisition, and RBI-mandated filings. We
ensure every step is handled with precision, so you can focus on your investments.
Foreign individuals and OCBs face significant hurdles during onboarding and compliance due to
stringent Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) requirements, which
demand extensive documentation and frequent updates. The regulatory landscape is constantly
evolving, with authorities such as SEBI and RBI regularly updating rules, making it challenging to stay
compliant. Language barriers, differences in documentation standards, and the need for beneficial
ownership disclosures further complicate the process. Furthermore, global initiatives like FATCA and
CRS require detailed tax disclosures, while data privacy laws restrict cross-border information
sharing. Geopolitical tensions, such as sanctions or trade restrictions arising from conflicts or
diplomatic disputes, can suddenly alter eligibility criteria or increase scrutiny, making onboarding a
complex and dynamic challenge for foreign investors and entities. iFinserve proactively addresses
these challenges on your behalf and provides regular updates, enabling you to make well-informed
decisions with confidence.

Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) Execution &amp; Liquidation: Get the best out of your ESOPs
with our tailor-made expert assistance. Be it exercising options, liquidating, or even the repatriation of
funds, we make the process simple for you as well as corporate organizations. For people residing
abroad, as well as for OCBs (Overseas Corporate Bodies), exercising and liquidation of ESOPs
brings about its set of difficulties such as handling multiple taxation issues in the two countries
(employment country and residential country) as well as restrictions from currency control authorities
on converting their profits into domestic currency and remitting them to their countries. The FEMA Act
in India imposes stringent requirements on companies when it comes to reporting and remittance.
Further, DTAAs (Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements) are another area requiring proper
interpretation by legal and financial experts. The geopolitics in the form of unstable currency rates and
ever-changing DTAAs also affect the overall value of ESOPs.

Tax-Aligned Remittance &amp; Fund Repatriation:
All our remittances are entirely tax compliant, following all the regulatory changes in a timely manner,
thus reducing risks and improving efficiency.
Remittance of funds and repatriation of investment on behalf of overseas nationals and the overseas
corporate bodies is usually not an easy task due to multiple reasons. First of all, there is the issue of
seeking necessary permissions and filing paperwork, and finally there is the need for TDS to be done
correctly without any risk of penalty. Transactions of this kind are controlled by the FEMA act in India,
which entails that every transaction has to strictly adhere to all regulations and reporting standards.
There are other complications as well, for example, when determining the tax residency of the
individual. This problem becomes even harder to solve against the backdrop of the increasingly
complicated geopolitical situation, characterized by stricter control over the flow of money from one
country to another.
